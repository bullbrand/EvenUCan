## ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 onboard LED

The onboard LED is an RGB (WS2812-style) LED. It is on GPIO 48 (board v1.0) or GPIO 38 (board v1.1). Use rgbLedWrite(pin, red, green, blue) in Arduino-ESP32 3.x, or an ESPHome esp32_rmt_led_strip light with chipset ws2812. digitalWrite does nothing to it.
