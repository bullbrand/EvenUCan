# Privacy Policy — EvenUCan

**Last updated: November 2026**

EvenUCan runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

## What EvenUCan does not do

- It does not collect analytics or usage data
- It does not create user accounts
- It does not track you in any way
- It has no servers of its own

## AI features

The optional AI features (gadget ideas, automatic parts matching, code generation) send only the text you type to the AI provider **you have chosen and configured** — for example Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek or Ollama. This connection goes directly from your computer to that provider. EvenUCan does not see, log or relay it.

If you use Ollama, the AI runs entirely on your own machine with no internet connection at all.

## Your drawings and projects

All drawings, parts lists and project files are stored locally on your computer in the .3mf files you save. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
