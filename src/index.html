Parts Library

📏 Measure your own parts. Two parts with the same name and part number can still be different sizes, because each maker builds them a little differently, sometimes by several millimetres. The sizes in this list are a good starting point, but the box is designed from these sizes. If they do not match the part in your hand, the box may not fit it. So whenever you can, measure the part you have (with calipers, or with the 📏 Measure from photos button), compare it with the built-in part, and correct the size here.
